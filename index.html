Got Questions?

We've shared a series of our most common questions and answered them. If you need additional information or have other questions, never hesitate to reach out to us.

What areas do you serve?
We proudly serve Spring Hill and surrounding areas, including Spring Hill and about a 15 mile radius.
What's included in your Full Service plan?
Weekly skim, brush, vacuum, filter check, water testing, full chemical balance, and a written report of every visit.
Do you offer chemical-only service?
Yes — perfect for DIY pool owners. We handle the chemistry weekly while you handle the rest.
What is the "Green to Clean" service?
An intensive multi-day treatment to restore an algae-ridden pool to clear, swimmable water using premium shock and clarifiers.
Do you provide start-up services for new construction & upgraded pools?
Absolutely. We handle the chemical start-up for plaster, pebble, and resurfaced pools so the finish cures properly.
How much does weekly service cost?
Prices vary based on your pool's size and condition. Chemical Only service starts at $100+/month and Full Service starts at $140+/month.
Can you repair or install pool equipment?
Yes — pumps, filters, heaters, salt cells, automation, and lighting. We're certified with Hayward, Jandy, Pentair, and Zodiac.
What does your Drain & Clean service include?
Drain, acid-wash where needed, refill, and full chemical start-up to balance the new water.
Do I need to be home during service visits?
No — most of our customers aren't home. We just need gate access. We'll leave a service slip every visit.
How do I get started with service?
Fill out the form below or call us at (352) 678-4100. We'll schedule a free on-site estimate within a few days.
